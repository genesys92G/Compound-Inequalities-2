03 · Build the idea

What is a compound statement?

A compound statement combines two simple ideas using the logical connectors AND or OR. The connector tells us how to combine their solution sets.

AND Overlap & intersection

An inequality with AND requires both parts to be true. Keep the values where the two solution sets overlap.

Example · written together · interval notation
x > 1 AND x < 4

So, 1 < x < 4 and the interval is (1, 4).

OR Union of solutions

An inequality with OR requires at least one part to be true. Keep everything shaded by either inequality.

Example · interval notation
x ≤ −1 OR x > 3

The solution is (−∞, −1] ∪ (3, ∞).

Read, solve, represent.
